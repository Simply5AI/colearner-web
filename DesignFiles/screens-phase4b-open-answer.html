Your Answer
After a successful recall (quality >= 3), the SM-2 algorithm increases the review interval based on the repetition count. For the first success it sets interval to 1 day, second success to 6 days, and subsequent successes multiply the previous interval by the ease factor. The ease factor itself is also updated based on the quality of the recall.

Pass threshold: score ≥ 6/10. SM-2 quality mapping: sm2_quality_from_score(score) → min(5, round(score/2)).
Timer is optional — if enabled, auto-submit on expiry with current text (or empty = scored 0).
