Source Progression System

1
Beginner
Foundation Source — Build your learning habit
YouTube video capture, basic concept extraction, first recall sessions. The learner discovers how ambient capture works.
Explore Learn
Unlock: Sign up → Complete first extraction
2
Intermediate
Growth Source — Expand your capture sources
Browser extension (web articles), multi-source capture, SM-2 scheduling becomes active, review queue management.
Learn Grow
Unlock: 50+ recall attempts · 70%+ pass rate
3
Advanced
Mastery Source — Deep knowledge synthesis
Cross-concept connections, PDF/document capture, study group features, NCU multipliers active, advanced analytics.
Grow Excel
Unlock: 200+ recall attempts · 80%+ pass rate · 30-day streak

Section 05

Engagement States

Explore — The Guide
Reward curiosity and direction of thinking. Warm, encouraging feedback. NCU Multiplier: 1.0×
Active
Learn — The Train
Be precise — identify exactly what was missing and how to fix it. Structured feedback. NCU Multiplier: 2.5×
Default
Grow — The Mentor
Acknowledge good work, then challenge with application questions. Push further. NCU Multiplier: 5.0×
Unlocked
Excel — The Coach
Be exacting — expose the precise gap. No hand-holding. Master-level coaching. NCU Multiplier: 10.0×
Locked
